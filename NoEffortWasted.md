Some time ago, Hank Green, an amazingly interesting being of a human posted a video called [No Effort is Wasted](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AgBaU9mmceQ). He emphasized on the importance of all the small experiences that we cumulatively gain throughout our lifetimes. In essence, it's a praise of what we call "side quests" these days. 
## Growth
He discusses personal growth and how we are shaped by our experiences in life-
## Junk Drawer
In the video, he compared the way we gather all these different experiences as if we were putting stuff in a 'junk drawer'. You may not think about what goes in there for long and it may not matter much to you in the moment, but that 'junk' we accumulate in our very own personal drawers are always there, exactly how we've left them. In the future, maybe a few days, weeks, months or even years, we'll have a peculiar need; A time will surely come when we must take on tasks that won't exactly line up with what we do every day. Perhaps you'll get into an endeavor that requires you to be versatile in your sources. And when that time comes, you'll look back in that little drawer that felt like a bunch of junk and realize just how well the smallest of things you learned may serve you.
